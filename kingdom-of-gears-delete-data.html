Kingdom of Gears — Delete your data

Developer: A.R_Games · App: Kingdom of Gears (Google Play)

Kingdom of Gears has no accounts and no sign-in, and A.R_Games does not run any server that stores information about you. Your game progress is kept on your phone. This page explains how to delete it, and how to delete the advertising data that Google's ad service (AdMob) uses.

1. Delete your game progress

  1. Open Kingdom of Gears.
  2. Tap Settings → Reset progress and confirm.

This permanently deletes your levels, troops, gold, gems and settings, on your phone and in the copy kept in your phone's Android backup. Uninstalling the game also removes it from your phone; to remove the Android backup copy too, open your phone's Settings → Google → Backup and delete the app data backup there. Gems and passes bought with real money cannot be restored after a reset.

2. Delete advertising data

The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, which uses your phone's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions and crash/diagnostic data. To delete or reset it:

  1. On your phone open Settings → Google → All services → Ads (on some phones: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  2. Tap Delete advertising ID (or Reset advertising ID).

Data Google keeps is handled under the Google Privacy Policy, where you can also make requests to Google directly.

3. Ask us

For any other request, email argames.support@gmail.com with the subject “Kingdom of Gears data deletion”. We answer within 30 days. We do not keep any personal data about you on our side, so there is nothing to retain after a request.
