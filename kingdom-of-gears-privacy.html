Kingdom of Gears — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Kingdom of Gears (“the game”) is made by A.R_Games, an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This page explains what information the game uses and why. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location, and the game has no account or sign-in.

1. Your game progress

Your levels, troops, gold, gems and settings are stored on your phone. On Android, a copy of this save file is included in your phone's normal Android backup to your own Google account (if backup is turned on in your phone's settings), so your progress can come back after reinstalling or on a new phone. We cannot see or access this backup. To delete your progress, use Settings → Reset progress in the game (this also replaces the backed-up copy). Uninstalling removes the game from your phone, but Android may keep its backup in your Google account and restore it if you install the game again; you can delete app backups in your phone's Google backup settings.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows optional rewarded ads — only when you choose to watch one for a reward. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game automatically collects and shares with Google:

DataExamples
Device and advertising identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID
IP addressmay be used to estimate general location (country/region)
Interactions with ads and the appapp launches, taps, ad views
Diagnostic informationapp performance, crash and hang information

This data is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other places where it is required, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent form; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases (Google Play)

Gem packs and the monthly passes are bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The game only receives a purchase confirmation from Google Play so it can give you what you bought. Passes renew monthly until you cancel them in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not run our own servers that collect information about you; the game works on your phone.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools other than what the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects as described above.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
